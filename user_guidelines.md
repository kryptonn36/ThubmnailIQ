# ThumbnailIQ — User Guide

Welcome! ThumbnailIQ helps YouTube creators score, compare, and improve their video
thumbnails using real competitor data and AI analysis — before they hit publish.
This guide walks through every feature available in the app and how to use it.

---

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — go to the homepage and click **Get Started Free** (or
   **Log In** if you already have an account).
2. Sign up with your **full name, email, and a password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. A **workspace** is created for you automatically — there's nothing extra to set
   up. You're immediately the workspace **Owner**.
4. You'll land on your **Dashboard**, which shows your usage stats and quick links
   to every tool.

If you're ever logged out unexpectedly, just log back in from `/login` — sessions
simply expire after a while and don't affect your saved data.

---

## 2. Dashboard (Home)

Your dashboard is the starting point and shows:

- **Analyses this month** vs. your plan's monthly limit
- **Average score** and **best score** across your completed analyses
- **Current plan** with a quick link to upgrade
- A **usage bar** that turns amber when you're running low on your monthly quota
- Four **quick-action tiles**: Analyze a Thumbnail, Generate Video Ideas, Track
  Competitors, and Viral Thumbnail DB
- Your **recent analyses**, each showing a thumbnail preview and score badge

---

## 3. Analyzing a Thumbnail

This is the core feature of ThumbnailIQ.

**How to use it:**
1. Go to **New Analysis** (from the dashboard tile or the sidebar).
2. Drag and drop your thumbnail image, or click to browse (**PNG/JPG**, 1280×720
   recommended).
3. Enter the **target keyword** your video is competing for (e.g. "how to lose
   weight fast").
4. Click **Analyze Thumbnail**.

Your thumbnail is scored against real (or synthetic, in demo mode) competitor
results for that keyword. Processing usually takes just a few seconds.

### Reading your results

Once complete, the analysis page shows:

- **ThumbnailIQ Score (0–100)** — an overall score with a colored gauge
- **Six sub-scores**: Visibility, Contrast, Attention, Mobile, Branding, and
  Curiosity — each showing how your thumbnail performs on that dimension
- **Keyword relevance warning** — if your thumbnail doesn't visually match your
  keyword, you'll see a note explaining that this hurt your score
- **SERP Preview** — a mockup of how your thumbnail looks next to competitors in
  YouTube search results
- **Computer-vision breakdown** — detected faces, text, colors, contrast, and
  clutter, compared against the competitor average
- **Niche benchmark** — how your score stacks up against others targeting the
  same keyword
- **Suggestions** — a ranked list of specific, actionable improvements
- **Competitor grid** — the actual competitor thumbnails used for scoring, with
  their scores and your rank among them
- **Track Performance** — after you publish the real video, come back and enter
  its actual CTR (from YouTube Studio) to see how it compares to your predicted
  score
- **Export PDF** — click to print/save the analysis as a PDF report
- **Add Version** — upload another variant of the thumbnail to compare side by
  side (see below)

---

## 4. Compare Thumbnails

Use this when you have multiple thumbnail options for the same video and want to
know which one to publish.

1. Go to **Compare** in the sidebar.
2. Enter your target keyword.
3. Click **+ Add Thumbnails** and select up to **5 variants**.
4. Each one is scored automatically; results appear side by side so you can pick
   the winner.

You can also add extra versions directly from an existing analysis page using the
**+ Add Version** button.

---

## 5. Video Ideas

Not sure what to make next? Use this to get AI-generated video concepts.

1. Go to **Ideas** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a keyword or topic (e.g. "crypto trading for beginners").
3. Click **Generate Ideas**.

You'll get a set of ideas, each with a **title, hook, content format** (How-To,
Listicle, Story, Challenge, Comparison, Case Study, Reaction), and an estimated
**CTR potential**. Click **Use this idea →** on any card to jump straight into a
new analysis pre-filled with that title.

---

## 6. Tracking Competitors

Keep an eye on how competitors' thumbnails evolve over time.

1. Go to **Tracking** in the sidebar.
2. Choose to track either a **Keyword** or a specific **Channel ID**.
3. Set how often it should check (in hours, up to every 7 days).
4. Click **Add Tracking Job**.

Your tracked items appear in a list showing their status and when they were last
checked. A background job periodically re-checks each one for changes.

---

## 7. Viral Thumbnail Database

Browse a searchable gallery of high-performing thumbnails for inspiration.

1. Go to **Database** in the sidebar.
2. Filter by **niche** (Gaming, Finance, Fitness, Beauty, Tech, Education,
   Entertainment), **minimum score**, and whether the thumbnail **has a face**.
3. Browse results, each showing the thumbnail, its score, channel, niche, and view
   count.

---

## 8. Brand Kit

Set your channel's visual identity so ThumbnailIQ can tailor its suggestions.

1. Go to **Brand Kit** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a **primary and secondary color** (or choose a quick preset palette), and
   a **brand font**.
3. Preview your branding live before saving.
4. Click **Save Brand Kit**.

---

## 9. Team Collaboration

Invite others to work in your workspace.

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a colleague's email and choose a role:
   - **Editor** — can create and view analyses
   - **Viewer** — can only view results
3. Click **Send Invite**.

The person who registered the workspace is always the **Owner** and has full
access.

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar to view your profile (name and email) and to

## Tips for Best Results

- Use a keyword that closely matches what your actual video is about — a
  mismatched keyword lowers your relevance score and rank.
- Upload thumbnails at **1280×720** for the most accurate analysis.
- Check the **Mobile** sub-score carefully — most viewers browse YouTube on
  phones, where small text and clutter hurt the most.
- Come back after publishing to log your **real CTR** — over time this helps you
  see how well the ThumbnailIQ score predicts real-world performance.
